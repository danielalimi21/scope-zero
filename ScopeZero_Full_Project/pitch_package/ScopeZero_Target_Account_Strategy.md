# ScopeZero Target Account Strategy

## Best First Customer Profile

Prioritize companies that already have:

- internal AI agents or AI platform teams
- regulated or sensitive data
- security teams that care about auditability
- tool/API/MCP workflows
- large SaaS and cloud footprint
- budget for security pilots

Avoid first:

- small businesses
- companies with no internal agent work
- buyers who only want generic chatbot governance
- teams that cannot give technical feedback

## Best Roles

Primary:

- CISO
- Head of AI Platform
- VP Security Engineering
- Head of Identity Security
- Head of Cloud Security
- SOC / Detection Engineering lead

Secondary:

- GRC leader
- Data security leader
- AppSec leader
- Corporate development at cyber companies
- Cyber-focused VC partner

## Highest-Probability Wedges

1. MCP/tool-call gateway for internal agents.
2. Prompt-injection-driven data egress prevention.
3. Agent identity and mission registry.
4. SIEM evidence for agent actions.
5. Approval gates for high-impact autonomous actions.

## How To Use The CSV

Start with 30 accounts:

- 10 regulated enterprises
- 10 AI/platform-heavy software companies
- 10 cyber/security platform companies

Send personalized notes. The first ask is not "buy my product." The first ask is:

> "Would you give 20 minutes of blunt feedback on whether this is a real problem?"

Measure:

- replies
- calls booked
- "we have this problem"
- "can you integrate with X?"
- "can we test it?"

That is the signal.
